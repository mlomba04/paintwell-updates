Paintwell Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Paintwell is a drawing and painting app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. Paintwell has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party crash reporting service. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Internet use

Paintwell may use an internet connection for:

  • Google Play purchases. If you buy Paintwell Pro, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. Paintwell never sees your card or payment details. To check whether Pro is unlocked, the app asks Google Play for your purchase status and keeps a small “Pro unlocked” flag on your device. Google’s handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The in-app brush catalog. When you browse or install a brush from the catalog, the app downloads publicly posted catalog files, preview images and brush packs over HTTPS from a file host we publish. Those downloads do not include your drawings or any personal information you type into the app. The file host may see a normal web request (such as an IP address), the way any website would.

The version of Paintwell distributed on Google Play receives app updates only through Google Play. It does not download or install app update files itself.

Your artwork and settings

  • Drawings, projects, custom brushes, palettes and preferences are saved only on your device, in Paintwell’s private app storage.
  • When you export or share, the file goes only where you send it (for example your photo gallery or another app you pick in Android’s share sheet). Paintwell never uploads your work anywhere.
  • Images you import are read only to place them on your canvas or in a reference window.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage. Images you exported to your gallery are kept.

Crash reports

If Paintwell crashes or stops responding, it saves a short technical report on your device only, in its private app storage. A report contains the app version, device model, Android version, screen size, free memory, basic app state (current tool, layer count, canvas size) and the technical error details. It does not contain your drawings or any personal information.

  • Reports are never uploaded automatically. They are sent only if you choose to share one, for example by tapping Send report, which opens Android’s share sheet so you pick the app and the recipient.
  • You can view, share or delete reports at any time in Settings › Crash reports. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Children

Paintwell does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not collect, share or sell personal data.
